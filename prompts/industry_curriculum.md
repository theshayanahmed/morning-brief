# Industry & Operations Curriculum

For Shayan as a senior commercial/strategy executive at MGH Group —
covers ocean shipping, air freight & aviation, customs, trade compliance,
industry economics, and commercial/strategic concepts relevant to running
a logistics conglomerate.

Track coverage in state.curriculum_progress per subtopic: count of times
tested + last_seen date + difficulty level reached (1=basic,
2=intermediate, 3=advanced, 4=edge-case).

Selection rules:
• Prioritise subtopics not seen in 14+ days
• Within a subtopic, ratchet difficulty up over successive sessions
• Don't test the same subtopic two days in a row
• Aim for full syllabus coverage every ~4 weeks
• Mix difficulty levels in a session — not all 5 basic, not all 5 edge-case

═══════ SYLLABUS ═══════

## 1. INCOTERMS 2020
EXW, FCA, FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU, DDP. Risk transfer point,
cost responsibility split, insurance obligation, mode-of-transport
restrictions. Diff between 2010 and 2020 (DAT→DPU, DDP/DAP nuances).

## 2. Documentation
Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin (general vs
preferential under FTA), inspection certificates (SGS, BV, Intertek),
phytosanitary, fumigation, dangerous goods declaration, IMO declaration,
shipper's letter of instruction, VGM declaration.

## 3. Bill of Lading
Master BL vs House BL. NVOCC vs VOCC. Negotiable (order BL) vs
non-negotiable (straight BL). Seaway BL, Express release, Telex release.
Switch BL (when/why/risks). Surrender procedure. To-order endorsement.

## 4. Customs & duty
HS code structure (chapter/heading/subheading + national digits). Valuation
methods (transaction, identical, similar, deductive, computed, fallback).
Duty calc: ad valorem vs specific. Anti-dumping & countervailing duties.
Drawback. Bonded warehouse vs FTZ vs SEZ. ATA carnet. Tariff engineering.

## 5. Vessel types & capacities
Container ships (Feeder, Panamax, Post-Panamax, Neo-Panamax, ULCV/Megamax).
Bulk (Handysize, Handymax/Supramax, Panamax, Capesize, VLOC). Tankers
(MR, LR1/LR2, Aframax, Suezmax, VLCC, ULCC). Ro-Ro, reefer, breakbulk,
project cargo, heavy-lift, LNG/LPG. Ice class.

## 6. Port operations
Terminal handling charge (THC, DTHC, OTHC). Demurrage vs detention vs
storage — who charges, when the clock starts/stops. Free time. Container
yard vs container freight station. Dwell time. Berth productivity (moves/hr).
Port congestion premiums.

## 7. Freight pricing components
Base rate. BAF/FAF (bunker adj). CAF (currency adj). LSS (low sulphur).
PSS (peak season). GRI (general rate increase). ECA (emission control area).
CIC (container imbalance). War risk. Suez/Panama transit. THC, DTHC, ISPS,
documentation fee, telex release fee, VGM fee, AMS/ENS/ICS2 filing fees.

## 8. Liner trade & alliances
Current alliance structure (Gemini Cooperation: Maersk + Hapag — from Feb
2025; Premier Alliance: ONE + HMM + Yang Ming; Ocean Alliance: CMA CGM +
COSCO + OOCL + Evergreen; MSC standalone). Sailing schedules,
transshipment hubs (Singapore, Colombo, Tanjung Pelepas, Salalah, Jebel Ali).
Slot charter, VSA, vessel sharing.

## 9. Air freight operations
IATA framework. ULDs (AKE, PMC, AKH dimensions). Chargeable weight (greater
of actual or volumetric, divisor 6000 cm³/kg). Master AWB vs House AWB.
Consolidator vs direct. Dangerous Goods Regulations (IATA DGR) — class
divisions, packing groups, ERG codes. CASS settlement.

## 9b. Aircraft types & cargo carriers
Freighters (production vs converted): 747-8F, 747-400F, 777F, 767F, A330F,
A350F, MD-11F. Bellyhold capacity on widebodies (777-300ER, A350-1000,
A380). Payload-range tradeoffs. Major dedicated cargo airlines (FedEx,
UPS, Atlas, Cargolux, Cathay Cargo, Korean Air Cargo, Lufthansa Cargo,
Emirates SkyCargo, Qatar Cargo, Turkish Cargo, China Airlines Cargo,
Saudia Cargo). Integrators (FedEx, UPS, DHL Express) vs traditional
carriers. Cargo-only vs combination.

## 9c. Airport operations & cargo hubs
Slot constraints, curfews (FRA, LHR night-flight bans), bonded warehousing.
Major cargo hubs by tonnage: HKG, MEM (FedEx), SHA-PVG, ICN, ANC (technical
stop), DXB, DOH, FRA, LEJ (DHL), CDG, SIN, NRT, TPE, ORD, LAX. Cool-chain
infrastructure (pharma corridors). Airport-airline cargo terminal
operators. ULD pooling. e-AWB adoption. Truck feeders / RFS (road feeder
services).

## 10. Multimodal & inland
FCL vs LCL. CY/CY, CY/CFS, CFS/CY, CFS/CFS movements. Inland container
depots. Rail intermodal (US double-stack, EU intermodal corridors,
China-Europe rail / BRI). Last-mile drayage. Combined transport BL.

## 11. Trade lanes & geography
Major lanes: Asia-Europe, Transpacific, Transatlantic, intra-Asia,
Far East-Middle East-India. Chokepoints: Suez, Panama, Strait of Hormuz,
Bab el-Mandeb, Strait of Malacca, Bosphorus. Cape routing economics.
Northern Sea Route. Emerging corridors: IMEC, Middle Corridor, BRI.

## 12. FTAs & trade preferences
Bangladesh-relevant: EU EBA (Everything But Arms), GSP+ (when applicable),
US GSP status, India-BD bilateral, SAFTA, BIMSTEC, RCEP (BD not member —
implications). LDC graduation timeline (BD graduates 2026) and impact on
trade preferences. Rules of origin (wholly obtained vs substantial
transformation vs % value-add).

## 13. Marine insurance
Institute Cargo Clauses A (all-risks), B (named perils), C (very limited).
General Average (York-Antwerp Rules). Salvage. Particular Average.
War risk & strikes cover. Insurable interest. Subrogation. CIF vs CIP
insurance levels (Clauses A vs C minimum under Incoterms 2020).

## 14. Risk & compliance
OFAC sanctions (SDN list, sectoral sanctions, secondary sanctions).
EU sanctions framework. UK OFSI. Russia sanctions specifics (price cap,
G7 ban, dual-use). Dual-use export controls (Wassenaar). Iran/DPRK/Cuba/
Syria. Anti-bribery (FCPA, UKBA). Forced labour (UFLPA — Xinjiang). KYC for
shipping (vessel ownership, ultimate beneficial owner, deceptive shipping
practices).

## 15. Industry economics & indices
SCFI (Shanghai), WCI (Drewry), CCFI, FBX (Freightos), Xeneta XSI. BDI
(Baltic Dry), BDTI/BCTI (tanker). TAC Index (air freight). Idle fleet %.
Orderbook-to-fleet ratio. Demolition / scrapping rates. Newbuild prices.
Charter rates (TC, voyage). Major forwarders by TEU/tonnage (Kuehne+Nagel,
DHL, DSV, Sinotrans, DB Schenker, Expeditors). Major carriers by capacity
(MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM, COSCO, Hapag-Lloyd, ONE, Evergreen, HMM, Yang Ming,
ZIM).

## 16. M&A and consolidation
Recent transformative deals: DSV–Schenker (2024-25, ~$15B), Maersk–LF
Logistics, Kuehne+Nagel–Apex, MSC's terminal acquisitions (Bolloré Africa
Logistics → AGL), CMA CGM expansion into integrators. Vertical integration
plays (carriers buying forwarders, forwarders buying tech). Digital
forwarder consolidation (Flexport, Forto, etc).

## 17. Commercial concepts for forwarders
RFQ/tender cycles. FAK (Freight All Kinds) vs commodity rates. MQC
(Minimum Quantity Commitment) and shortfall liability. NACs (Named Account
contracts). Service contracts vs spot. NRA (NVOCC service arrangements,
US-specific). FAFs (FMC tariff filings). Buy-rate vs sell-rate spread.
Contracted volume guarantees. Rate validity windows. Surcharge pass-through
mechanics.

## 18. Supply chain & logistics strategy
Push vs pull. Postponement strategies. Cross-dock vs warehouse-and-pick.
Omnichannel fulfilment. Just-in-time vs just-in-case (post-COVID rebalance).
Nearshoring/friendshoring vs reshoring. Bullwhip effect. Inventory days
(DSI). Network design (regional DCs, fulfilment centres). 3PL vs 4PL vs
5PL distinctions. Control tower concept.
